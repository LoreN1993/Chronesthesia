This is a Mental Time Line task developed in Opensesame with a Psychopy environment. The general aim of the task is to track the spatiotemporal dynamics of mouse movements, underlying the cognitive processes involved during the mental representations of subjective time (Chronesthesia) including Past (N), Present (P) and Future (F). In this task participants are told that they will see a horizontal line placed at the center of the screen, representing the average life span of a gender matched individual. The main scope of the task is to decide where on that line, the participants would place the start of their Birth (N), where is their Present (P) and where their Future begins, represented as letters on the screen.

The scientific research interest behind this task is dual and interwined: firstly we aim at obtain a discrete measure of the magnitude processing behind the transformation form the abstract Subjective Time representations domain to the concrete Spatial 2D horizontal domain. These discrete measures lead us to obtain several "Chronesthetic indicators" as a result of the distance in mm between the three markers, offering spatially extension windows as indicator of Subjective Time perception; secondly thorugh the online tracking of mouse movements we computes several mouse tracking indicators relying upon the Mousetrap library available in R.

The main research question is: The spatiotemporal dynamics of mouse movements and trajectories can predict the degree of magnitude processing in terms of 2D spatial extentions, of subjective time? In other words, mouse tracking the cognitive processes underlying subjective time representions, are reliable predictors of subjective time perception or chronesthesia?
